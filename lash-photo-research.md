# Round 9 — Lash photography research (Jul 2026)

## Trending lash styles (2026)
1. **Korean natural wispy** — dominant global trend. Soft spikes: short + slightly longer lash points, criss-cross texture, clean separation, "your lashes but dreamier". Lightweight, never spidery. Best for First Light (classic).
2. **Clean wet-look Korean sets** — narrow closed fans that look like freshly applied mascara; darker, sharper definition while lightweight. Glossy, sleek finish. Works for Full Gold (volume) styled modern, not dense Russian volume.
3. **Soft anime/manga** — refined doll-eye clusters, spaced spikes; playful. Not one of Oriana's services, skip.
4. **Korean lash lift (glueless)** — biggest lift trend: clean root lift, natural curl root-to-tip, "born with it". Perfect for The Lift.

## How top studios photograph lash results
- Extreme close-up of ONE eye, eye open or 3/4 closed, shot slightly from above/side so the lash fan silhouette reads clearly against skin
- Macro lens sharpness on the lash line: each fan/spike individually visible = communicates fullness honestly
- Soft diffused beauty lighting (ring light / softbox), luminous clean skin, groomed brow framing the top
- Warm neutral / golden tones; shallow depth of field; skin has texture but is flattering, not raw
- Editorial polish: it looks intentional and "shot for a campaign", not a phone snap on the treatment bed

## Art direction for Oriana v4 photos (gradient-grain brand, warm peach/gold, Southeast Asian models)
- **First Light (classic)** → Korean natural wispy: soft spikes, airy separation, natural length
- **Full Gold (volume)** → clean wet-look volume: narrow dark fans, noticeably fuller, glossy definition
- **The Lift** → Korean glueless lift look: bare lashes lifted from root, elegant curl, no extensions, fresh dewy eye
- **The Return (infill)** → a refreshed wispy set, immaculate lash line, mid-wear perfection restored
- Consistent series: same warm editorial light, single eye macro crop, brow in frame, luminous SEA skin with subtle real texture (pores/faint freckles per brand standard), square 1:1
